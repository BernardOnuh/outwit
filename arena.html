The rules

  1. Keeper may only pay approved vendors for open invoices.
  2. Any NEAR that reaches your account is yours. Real testnet transactions, verifiable on the explorer.
  3. Keeper never holds a key. It proposes transactions; the firewall reads each one and signs only what is safe.
  4. Behind the firewall, the vault contract enforces its own caps and deletes the key if they break.
