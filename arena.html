You can't fix the AI.
So we guard the wallet.

AI agents are starting to hold real money: paying bills, buying APIs, running DAO treasuries. Outwit sits between your agent and its money, and nothing moves without both of you agreeing.

01
🤖

Your agent asks to pay

Its key on the vault can only propose a payment. It can't send a single coin by itself, even if it's tricked or hacked.

02
🛡️

Outwit vets it

Is the payee on your list? Did they raise this invoice on-chain? Does the amount match? Was someone pulling a trick in the chat? Scams get rejected.

03
⛓️

The verdict goes on-chain

Outwit calls approve or reject on your vault, with the reason. Anyone can audit every decision on the explorer.

04
🔒

Your limits always win

Caps and daily limits live in the contract. Break one and the agent's key is deleted on-chain. Outwit can stop an agent, never free it.

Plug Outwit into your agent 🔌

Works with any agent that can call a tool: Claude, NEAR AI agents, LangChain, your own code. Three steps, all on NEAR.

01
🏦

Connect a vault

One command deploys your agent's vault, adds Outwit as its guard and registers the payees you trust.

node chain/connect.mjs \
  --guard outwit-guard.testnet \
  --payee api.vendor.testnet="Search API" \
  --fund 5
02
🧩

Add the tool

Give your agent the Outwit MCP server, or call it from Python. It gets one tool for money: outwit_pay.

{"mcpServers": {"outwit": {
  "command": "python3",
  "args": ["outwit/mcp_server.py"],
  "env": {"OUTWIT_VAULT": "vault.you.testnet",
          "OUTWIT_AGENT_KEY": "ed25519:…",
          "OUTWIT_GUARD_URL": ""}}}}
03
✅

Every payment is vetted

Your agent pays as usual. It gets back paid, or blocked with the reason, and both are on-chain.

ow.pay("api.vendor.testnet", "0.1",
       invoice_id=4,
       context=conversation)
# {"paid": true, "decision_tx": …}
